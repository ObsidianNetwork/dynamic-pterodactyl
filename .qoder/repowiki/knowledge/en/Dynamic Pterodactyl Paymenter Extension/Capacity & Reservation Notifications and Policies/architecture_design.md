Two sub-namespaces under the DynamicPterodactyl extension: `Notifications/` holds queueable Laravel Notification classes (`CapacityAlertNotification`, `ReservationShortfallNotification`) that render `MailMessage` payloads for alert delivery; `Policies/` provides `ResourceReservationPolicy` which gates access to `ResourceReservation` model actions via Filament's admin panel bypass in `before()` and per-record ownership checks in `view`, `cancel`, `extend`, `confirm`. The policy is consumed by Filament resources and service-layer calls that authorize against `ResourceReservation`. Notifications are dispatched independently of policies — they carry plain DTOs (alert config, breached thresholds, reservation snapshots) and do not depend on authorization. Both notification classes implement `Queueable`; `ReservationShortfallNotification` additionally implements `ShouldQueue` with `afterCommit = true` so it fires after the transaction commits.
